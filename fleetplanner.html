1

Install the Browser Extension

Download and unzip fleet-export-extension.zip.

Chrome / Edge / Brave / Opera:
Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions, opera://extensions).
Enable Developer mode (toggle in the top right corner).
Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.

Firefox:
Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
Click Load Temporary Add-on.
Select the manifest.json file from the unzipped folder.

2

Export Your Hangar

Go to robertsspaceindustries.com/account/pledges and log in.
An export bar appears at the bottom of the page.
Click Start Export — it automatically pages through your entire hangar and downloads hangar_export.csv.

3

Import Into the Planner

Drag hangar_export.csv onto the drop zone above, or click to browse.
Ships, CCUs, paints, and gear are parsed automatically with melt values and current store prices.
You can also import a previously saved .json plan to continue where you left off.

Privacy: Everything runs in your browser. No data is uploaded anywhere. The org export contains only ship names — no melt values, no account info.
Note on prices: Store prices are fetched from the Star Citizen Wiki during export. Newly released ships may take a day or two before the wiki adds their price. If you need updated prices later, just re-export from RSI and re-import here — your CCU chains will be preserved.
